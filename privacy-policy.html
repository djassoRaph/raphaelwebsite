Privacy Policy

Operated by Roots Time Corporation (Raphaël Reck), based in France. What we collect, what we don't, and your rights.

What we collect

If you use the contact form, we collect your name, email address, the enquiry type you select, and whatever you write in the message. That's it. It goes straight to an inbox by email. We don't store it in a database, sell it, share it with anyone else, or use it for anything other than replying to you.

Cookies

We don't use tracking cookies. The only thing saved in your browser is a light/dark theme preference and whether you've dismissed the cookie banner, both stored locally on your device (localStorage), never sent to us or anyone else.

Third parties

Icons are loaded from a CDN (cdnjs.cloudflare.com). That's the only external request this site makes on your behalf.

Your rights

Since we're EU/France-based, you have the right to ask what we have on you, correct it, or have it deleted. Given we only ever have what you typed into a contact form, this is usually a five-minute email away: contact@raphaelreck.com.

Changes

If anything here changes, this page will change with it. No newsletter, no notification, just check back if you care.
